# Missing Content for Great Burrow GM Binder

## Overview
This document analyzes content from various Great Burrow files that could be added to the `mousehattan_gm_binder.md` to make it more comprehensive for GMs.

## Major Missing Elements

### 1. Species Details and Cultural Information
**Source: species.md**

While the GM binder includes basic archetypes for many species, it's missing significant cultural depth:

- **Voles**: Listed in Mousehattan demographics (15% - 4,200) but no archetype
- **Hamsters**: Listed in Mousehattan demographics (10% - 2,800) but no archetype
- **Ferrets**: Have an archetype but missing the detailed cultural information about being "hyperactive deal-makers" and business "kits"
- **Pine Martens**: Mentioned in external relations but no archetype for these western traders
- **Corvids**: Critical to The Branchx but only briefly mentioned - missing their alien intelligence nature and complex protection rackets
- **Mixed Heritage Rules**: The species.md has extensive content on cross-species relationships, adoption, and found families
- **Regional Variations**: Urban vs rural differences within species
- **Disabilities and Accommodations**: Completely missing from GM binder
- **Aging and Lifecycle Differences**: Species longevity variations and their social impacts

### 2. Burrow-Specific Details
**Sources: Burrows/ folder files**

Each burrow file contains rich detail not fully captured in the GM binder:

#### Mousehattan Missing:
- Detailed districts (only basic overview provided)
- Specific crime family territories and operations
- The Weasel Network as secondary criminal organization
- Historical giant encounters and their lasting impact
- Food culture details (47 ways to prepare acorns, fermented root beer)
- Specific locations like The Squeakeasy, Moonlight Market, The Rat's Nest

#### Other Burrows:
- Similar level of detail missing for The Branchx, Hives, Lin's Brook, and Scurry Island
- Specific neighborhoods, local politics, cultural tensions unique to each

### 3. Organized Crime Details
**Source: misc/organized_crime_underworld.md**

The GM binder has basic crime family information but misses:
- The Commission (cross-burrow criminal coordination)
- Detailed operations of secondary syndicates (Downstream Boys, Wet Whiskers, etc.)
- Criminal codes of conduct and justice systems
- Money laundering operations and underground markets
- Species-specific criminal specializations
- Law enforcement corruption levels by burrow

### 4. Infrastructure and Daily Life
**Sources: misc/ folder files**

Missing specialized content:
- Master Timeline (230 years of detailed history)
- Food and cuisine details by culture
- Religious and spiritual traditions beyond basic mentions
- Pets and domestic animals (completely absent)
- External relations with specific trading partners
- Transportation infrastructure beyond basics

### 5. GM Tools and Resources
**Source: campaign_material/ folder**

While marked to ignore, contains useful GM material:
- Extensive campaign ideas (full campaigns, short campaigns, one-shots)
- Adventure themes organized by burrow
- Detailed adventure hooks with multiple complexity levels
- Campaign theme guidance (Urban Fantasy vs Political Intrigue vs Survival Horror)

### 6. Mechanical Elements
**Source: Comparing genesys_rules.md to GM binder**

Most mechanics seem included, but some gaps:
- Titan-touched rules could use the "acquiring titan-touched status" rules
- More detailed weather/seasonal mechanics
- Complete vehicle stats (only basic framework shown)
- Detailed reputation system consequences

## Recommendations for Addition

### Priority 1 - Critical Missing Elements
1. **Complete Species Guide**: Add all missing species archetypes and cultural details
2. **Organized Crime Expansion**: Add The Commission, criminal codes, and burrow-specific operations
3. **Mixed Heritage Rules**: Important for character creation and social dynamics
4. **Corvid Intelligence**: Critical for The Branchx and unique to setting

### Priority 2 - Enhance Play Experience  
1. **Burrow Districts**: Add specific neighborhoods and locations for each burrow
2. **Historical Timeline**: Add key events for campaign context
3. **Food and Daily Life**: Cultural details that bring setting alive
4. **Adventure Hooks**: Organize by complexity and burrow

### Priority 3 - Nice to Have
1. **External Relations**: Detailed trading partners and bandit groups
2. **Religious Traditions**: Expanded spiritual practices
3. **Pets and Domestic Animals**: Minor but flavorful
4. **Disability and Aging**: Progressive inclusivity elements

## Implementation Notes

- Current GM binder is ~55k tokens, already quite large
- Consider creating a "GM Companion" document for overflow content
- Prioritize content that directly impacts gameplay over pure flavor
- Focus on mechanics and stats over narrative descriptions
- Keep organized crime content together as it spans all burrows
- Ensure all species mentioned in demographics have playable archetypes

## Conclusion

The current GM binder is comprehensive but could benefit from:
1. Complete species coverage with cultural details
2. Expanded organized crime networks and codes
3. Specific burrow locations and neighborhoods  
4. Mixed heritage and inclusivity rules
5. More adventure hooks organized by type/length

This would transform it from a good foundation into a truly complete campaign guide.

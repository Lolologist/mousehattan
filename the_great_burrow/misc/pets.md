Forest Pets
Since dogs and cats are potentially playable races (though rare), the creatures of The Great Burrow keep different companions:
Common Pets:

Crickets: The most popular pet - they sing, they're easy to keep, and kids love racing them. Different breeds produce different songs.
Ladybugs: Considered good luck, kept in small terrariums. The spotted patterns are bred for beauty.
Caterpillars: Temporary pets that children raise until they transform. There's always drama when the butterfly leaves.
Jumping Spiders: For the more adventurous. They're trainable, surprisingly affectionate, and excellent pest control.
Pill Bugs (Roly-Polies): The "starter pet" for young mammals. Hardy, harmless, and entertaining.

Status Pets:

Fancy Beetles: Imported species with iridescent shells. Mousehattan elites pay fortunes for Japanese varieties.
Trained Moths: Night-flying message carriers for the wealthy. Beautiful but high-maintenance.

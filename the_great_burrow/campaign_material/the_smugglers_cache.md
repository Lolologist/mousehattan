# **The Smuggler's Cache**
*A One-Shot Adventure for The Great Burrow*

## **Adventure Overview**

**Duration:** 3-4 hours  
**Players:** 3-5 characters  
**Tone:** Urban exploration with social intrigue and action  
**Themes:** Competing interests, moral choices, urban survival, species cooperation

### **The Hook**
While exploring an abandoned warehouse in the Greenbank district of Lin's Brook (recently gentrified waterfront), the characters discover a hidden cache of valuable contraband. However, they're not the only ones looking for it - crime family enforcers and desperate smugglers are also converging on the location, all while trying to avoid the attention of burrow guards who would arrest everyone involved.

### **The Stakes**
- **Personal:** The cache contains enough valuable goods to solve immediate financial problems
- **Social:** Getting caught could ruin reputations and relationships
- **Political:** The contraband reveals corruption in the ferry system and trade regulations
- **Community:** The resolution affects ongoing gentrification and working-class displacement

---

## **Background**

### **The Smuggling Operation**
Three months ago, **Finn Shellcracker** (otter, ferry operator) was running a small smuggling operation, moving luxury goods from Mousehattan to Scurry Island without paying the proper tariffs. His contact was **Whiskers McGillicuddy** (mouse, "import/export specialist") who had connections with the Longtail crime family. When a burrow guard investigation got too close, Finn panicked and hid his most valuable shipment in an abandoned warehouse, planning to retrieve it once things cooled down.

### **The Complication**
Finn was arrested two weeks ago on unrelated charges (drunk and disorderly during an otter slide race). In jail, he let slip details about the cache to his cellmate **Rusty Crowbar** (rat, small-time criminal). Rusty has since been released and is planning to steal the cache. Meanwhile, Whiskers has been pressuring the Longtail family to find the missing goods, leading to **Salvatore "Sal" Longtail** (rat, crime family enforcer) being assigned to recover the shipment.

### **The Discovery**
The characters discover the cache through one of several possible means:
- **Accidental Discovery:** Exploring the abandoned warehouse for shelter, work, or curiosity
- **Hired Investigation:** Whiskers hires them to find the missing goods (without mentioning crime family involvement)
- **Tip from Informant:** A contact provides information about valuable goods hidden in Greenbank
- **Following Suspicious Activity:** Noticing Rusty or Sal casing the warehouse

## **Key NPCs**

### **Finn Shellcracker** *(Otter, Ferry Operator)*
**Personality:** Nervous, talkative, tries to be helpful but makes things worse  
**Motivation:** Get out of jail and retrieve his cache without getting killed  
**Speech Pattern:** Fluid, water metaphors, apologetic, "Listen, I can explain..."  
**Key Information:** Location of cache, combination to lock, details about what's inside  
**Current Status:** In Lin's Brook jail, available for questioning if characters visit

**Read-Aloud Introduction:** *A sleek brown otter sits hunched on the jail's stone bench, his usually glossy fur dulled and unkempt. His webbed paws fidget constantly, and his dark eyes dart nervously between you and the cell door. When he speaks, his voice carries the fluid cadence of his species but trembles with anxiety. "Listen, I can explain everything - well, most of it anyway. This whole mess just got away from me like a riptide, you know? One minute I'm running a simple side business, next thing the current's pulling me under and I'm sitting here while my whole life's work is hidden where the tide can't reach it. You seem like reasonable folk - maybe we can help each other navigate these choppy waters?"*

### **Whiskers McGillicuddy** *(Mouse, "Import/Export")*
**Personality:** Smooth-talking, well-dressed, nervous about crime family pressure  
**Motivation:** Recover goods to satisfy Longtail family and save his reputation  
**Speech Pattern:** Precise mouse diction, business jargon, "Let's discuss this professionally"  
**Key Information:** Value of goods, crime family involvement, pressure he's under  
**Location:** Mousehattan financial district, but will travel to meet characters

**Read-Aloud Introduction:** *A impeccably groomed mouse approaches, his gray fur perfectly combed and his tiny whiskers waxed to sharp points. He wears a miniature vest of fine cloth and carries himself with the practiced confidence of old Mousehattan money, though you notice his paws shake slightly as he adjusts his spectacles. His voice is cultured and precise, each word carefully chosen. "Good day. I am Whiskers McGillicuddy of McGillicuddy Import & Export - perhaps you've heard of our firm? I find myself in need of... discrete assistance with a matter of some delicacy. Let's discuss this professionally, shall we? Time, as they say in the financial district, is acorns, and I'm afraid both are in rather short supply at the moment."*

### **Salvatore "Sal" Longtail** *(Rat, Crime Family Enforcer)*
**Personality:** Professional, intimidating, surprisingly reasonable if treated with respect  
**Motivation:** Recover family property, maintain reputation, avoid unnecessary violence  
**Speech Pattern:** Confident rat speech, family metaphors, "Nothing personal, just business"  
**Key Information:** Crime family operations, consequences of theft, potential deals  
**Resources:** 2-3 rat associates, crime family backing, knowledge of underground

**Read-Aloud Introduction:** *A large, well-built rat emerges from the shadows, his dark fur immaculately groomed and his posture radiating quiet authority. Unlike the flashy new-money rats of Mousehattan, he dresses simply but expensively - quality speaks louder than ostentation. His tail, longer than most, moves with deliberate control, and his dark eyes assess you with professional interest rather than hostility. When he speaks, his voice is calm and measured, carrying the weight of unquestioned authority. "Good evening. I am Salvatore Longtail, and I represent certain... family interests in this matter. I understand there may be some confusion about ownership of certain goods. Nothing personal, you understand - just business. Perhaps we can resolve this like civilized creatures? The family appreciates cooperation, and we remember our friends."*

### **Rusty Crowbar** *(Rat, Small-time Criminal)*
**Personality:** Desperate, paranoid, quick to violence when cornered  
**Motivation:** Score big to pay off debts and get out of the city  
**Speech Pattern:** Street rat slang, nervous energy, "This is my ticket out"  
**Key Information:** Jail gossip, other criminal operations, desperation level  
**Resources:** Improvised weapons, knowledge of abandoned buildings, nothing to lose

**Read-Aloud Introduction:** *A scrawny rat with patchy fur and wild eyes skulks into view, clutching a makeshift weapon - a sharpened piece of metal that might once have been a crowbar. His clothes are mismatched and dirty, and he moves with the twitchy energy of someone who's been looking over his shoulder for too long. Scars crisscross his arms and face, telling stories of a hard life in the city's underbelly. His voice is rough and rapid, words tumbling over each other in his haste. "Well, well, what do we got here? More treasure hunters, eh? Listen up, this is MY score, see? Been planning this for weeks, and I ain't letting nobody muscle in on my ticket out of this rat-hole city. You seem like smart folk - walk away now and nobody gets hurt. This is my one shot at the big time, and I ain't going back to scraping for scraps!"*

### **Officer Marina Tidecaller** *(Otter, Burrow Guard)*
**Personality:** Dedicated, fair but strict, knows the neighborhood well  
**Motivation:** Keep the peace, investigate suspicious activity, protect community  
**Speech Pattern:** Authoritative otter speech, water metaphors, "What's going on here?"  
**Key Information:** Legal consequences, ongoing investigations, community concerns  
**Resources:** Legal authority, backup guards, knowledge of local crime

"What's going on here, citizens? I've had reports of suspicious activity in this area, and I can see why. ### **Granny Driftwood** *(Beaver, Local Resident)*
**Personality:** Elderly, observant, protective of neighborhood, suspicious of outsiders  
**Motivation:** Preserve community character, watch out for trouble  
**Speech Pattern:** Deliberate beaver speech, construction metaphors, "In my day..."  
**Key Information:** Neighborhood history, who's been around, what's normal  
**Location:** Lives across from warehouse, watches everything from her window

**Read-Aloud Introduction:** *An elderly beaver sits in her window, a steaming cup of bark tea in her weathered paws and a pair of well-used binoculars around her neck. Her gray-streaked fur is neatly groomed, and she wears a hand-knitted shawl that speaks of practical comfort over fashion. Her lodge is clearly well-maintained - a testament to beaver craftsmanship and pride. Her voice carries the deliberate cadence of her species, each word carefully considered like a well-placed timber. "Well now, what have we here? More strangers poking around that old warehouse, I see. In my day, creatures minded their own business and respected their neighbors' property. Got an honest look about you. Care to tell an old beaver what's really going on over there?"*

## **Key Locations**

### **1. The Abandoned Warehouse** *(Primary Location)*
**Description:** A three-story brick building on the Greenbank waterfront, formerly used for storing lumber. Now empty due to gentrification, with broken windows and a "For Lease" sign.

**Sensory Description:** *The warehouse greets you with the musty scent of old wood and damp stone, mixed with the salt tang of the nearby waterfront. Creaking timbers echo your footsteps as loose floorboards groan underfoot, while shafts of dusty sunlight filter through broken windows, casting dancing shadows across water-stained walls. The rhythmic lapping of waves against the dock below provides a constant backdrop, punctuated by the distant calls of dock workers and the occasional screech of rusty hinges. Your paws feel the gritty texture of sawdust and debris, while the air tastes of brine and decay - a monument to Lin's Brook's industrial past now surrendering to an uncertain future.*

**Layout:**
- **Ground Floor:** Open space with old lumber racks, loading dock, office
- **Second Floor:** Storage rooms, supervisor's office with good view of street
- **Basement:** Flooded during high tide, contains the hidden cache

**The Cache Location:** Behind a false wall in the basement, accessible only during low tide. Contains:
- 50 bottles of premium Hives honey liqueur (worth 500 acorns)
- Silk scarves from The Hives (worth 200 acorns)
- Rare spices from Coastal Tidepools (worth 300 acorns)
- Mousehattan luxury goods (worth 400 acorns)
- **Total Value:** 1,400 acorns (enough to buy a modest home)

**Challenges:**
- Timing with tides (cache only accessible at low tide)
- Structural damage (unsafe floors, loose boards)
- Multiple entrances (easy to be surprised)
- Visibility from street (Granny Driftwood watches)

### **2. Granny Driftwood's House** *(Observation Post)*
**Description:** Well-maintained beaver lodge built into the waterfront, with a perfect view of the warehouse. Granny sits by her window with tea and binoculars.

**Roleplay Opportunities:**
- Information gathering about recent activity
- Negotiating for her silence or cooperation
- Learning neighborhood history and concerns
- Getting warnings about approaching trouble

### **3. The Dockside Tavern** *(Information Hub)*
**Description:** Working-class otter bar where dock workers gather after shifts. Dim lighting, water-stained wood, competitive slide-racing stories.

**NPCs Present:**
- **Barkeep Splash** (otter): Knows everyone, trades information for drinks
- **Various Dock Workers:** Gossip about smuggling, crime families, recent arrests

**Information Available:**
- Finn's arrest and jailhouse gossip
- Recent crime family activity
- Smuggling routes and methods
- Community concerns about gentrification

### **4. Lin's Brook Jail** *(Optional Location)*
**Description:** Small holding facility built into a converted beaver dam. Secure but not maximum security.

**Visiting Finn:**
- Requires convincing Officer Tidecaller or paying small bribe
- Finn is eager to talk, provides cache details
- Other prisoners might overhear (future complications)
- Guards are suspicious of visitors

### **5. Greenbank Ferry Terminal** *(Escape Route)*
**Description:** Small dock with irregular ferry service to other parts of the city. Currently under renovation, creating hiding spots and obstacles.

**Tactical Considerations:**
- Multiple escape routes by water
- Construction equipment for cover
- Ferry schedules affect timing
- Officer Tidecaller patrols here regularly

## **Scene Breakdown**

### **Scene 1: The Discovery** *(30 minutes)*
**Location:** Abandoned Warehouse  
**Type:** Exploration and Investigation

**Setup:** Characters enter the warehouse through their chosen method. Describe the atmosphere - creaking wood, water sounds, urban decay mixed with gentrification pressure.

**Key Events:**
- Explore the building, noting structural damage and recent activity
- Discover signs that others have been here (footprints, disturbed dust)
- Find the basement entrance and realize it's tidal
- Locate the hidden cache (requires investigation or Finn's information)

**Complications:**
- Granny Driftwood spots them and calls out
- Structural hazards (loose floorboards, weak stairs)
- Tide timing creates urgency
- Signs that others are coming

**NPCs:** Granny Driftwood (if characters investigate her calls)

### **Scene 2: The Watchers** *(20 minutes)*
**Location:** Granny Driftwood's House  
**Type:** Social Encounter

**Setup:** Granny Driftwood has been watching the warehouse and knows something's up. She's protective of her neighborhood but also curious about what's happening.

**Roleplay Opportunities:**
- Learn about recent suspicious activity
- Negotiate for information or silence
- Understand community concerns about gentrification
- Get warnings about approaching trouble

**Possible Outcomes:**
- **Ally:** Granny helps watch for trouble and provides local knowledge
- **Neutral:** She stays out of it but won't actively help or hinder
- **Hostile:** She calls the guards if characters seem threatening

**Information Revealed:**
- Rusty has been casing the building for days
- Well-dressed mouse (Whiskers) was asking questions yesterday
- Several rats in nice clothes were in the area this morning
- Ferry schedules and tide times

### **Scene 3: The Competition Arrives** *(45 minutes)*
**Location:** Warehouse and surrounding area  
**Type:** Tense Negotiation with Combat Potential

**Setup:** Just as characters are accessing the cache (or planning to), both Rusty and Sal's crew arrive. This creates a three-way standoff with multiple possible resolutions.

**Participants:**
- **Characters:** Trying to claim or escape with the cache
- **Rusty:** Desperate, armed with improvised weapons, alone
- **Sal + 2 Associates:** Professional, well-equipped, prefer negotiation

**Possible Approaches:**
- **Negotiation:** Split the goods, work together, make deals
- **Intimidation:** Use reputation, connections, or threats
- **Deception:** Misdirection, false information, hidden agendas
- **Combat:** Last resort, but possible if negotiations fail

**Complications:**
- Tide is coming in, limiting time
- Granny Driftwood might call guards
- Structural damage makes fighting dangerous
- Cache is heavy and awkward to move

**Key Roleplay Moments:**
- Sal's professional approach vs. Rusty's desperation
- Moral choices about violence and theft
- Species dynamics and prejudices
- Community impact considerations

### **Scene 4: The Law Arrives** *(30 minutes)*
**Location:** Warehouse area  
**Type:** Social Encounter with Authority

**Setup:** Officer Tidecaller arrives to investigate reports of suspicious activity. How characters handle this depends on previous choices and current situation.

**Variables:**
- **What Granny Reported:** Ranges from "suspicious activity" to "armed criminals"
- **Current Situation:** Who's present, what's visible, how tense things are
- **Character Reputation:** Previous interactions with law enforcement

**Possible Approaches:**
- **Cooperation:** Work with officer to resolve situation legally
- **Deception:** Hide criminal activity, claim legitimate business
- **Bribery:** Offer money or favors for looking the other way
- **Escape:** Avoid confrontation entirely through stealth or misdirection

**Officer Tidecaller's Priorities:**
1. Community safety and peace
2. Investigating reported crimes
3. Understanding what's really happening
4. Avoiding paperwork if possible

**Outcomes Depend On:**
- How characters present themselves
- Whether violence has occurred
- Community impact and Granny's opinion
- Evidence of criminal activity

### **Scene 5: The Resolution** *(30 minutes)*
**Location:** Various  
**Type:** Consequences and Wrap-up

**Setup:** Based on previous choices, characters deal with the aftermath of their decisions.

**Possible Outcomes:**

**Cooperative Resolution:**
- Characters, Sal, and possibly Rusty work out a deal
- Goods are split or sold with profits shared
- Crime family gets their cut, everyone else benefits
- Community relationships are maintained or improved

**Criminal Success:**
- Characters escape with some or all of the cache
- Must deal with ongoing crime family pressure
- Reputation in criminal circles improves
- Legal heat and community suspicion increase

**Legal Resolution:**
- Officer Tidecaller mediates a legal solution
- Goods are returned to proper authorities
- Characters might receive reward or legal immunity
- Community standing improves, criminal contacts suffer

**Violent Outcome:**
- Combat has occurred, with consequences
- Injured parties need medical attention
- Legal investigation becomes serious
- Community trust is damaged
- Ongoing feuds and revenge plots

**Long-term Consequences:**
- Reputation changes with various factions
- Economic impact (money gained or lost)
- Relationship changes with NPCs
- Future adventure hooks established

## **Combat Encounters**

### **Optional Combat 1: Desperate Measures** *(If Negotiations Fail)*
**Participants:**
- **Rusty Crowbar:** Desperate rat with improvised weapons
- **Possible Allies:** None (fights alone)

**Tactics:** Rusty fights dirty, uses environment, tries to grab goods and run

**Environment:** Warehouse with structural hazards, multiple levels, water below

**Resolution:** Rusty surrenders if reduced to half health, tries to escape if possible

### **Optional Combat 2: Professional Disagreement** *(If Crime Family Turns Hostile)*
**Participants:**
- **Sal Longtail:** Professional enforcer with backup
- **2 Rat Associates:** Loyal but not suicidal

**Tactics:** Coordinated, professional, prefer intimidation to violence

**Environment:** Could be warehouse, street, or docks depending on scene flow

**Resolution:** Sal's crew withdraws if clearly outmatched, focuses on recovering goods over revenge

## **Treasure and Rewards**

### **The Cache Contents** *(Total Value: 1,400 acorns)*
- **50 bottles honey liqueur:** 10 acorns each, popular in Mousehattan
- **Silk scarves:** 20 acorns each, fashionable luxury items
- **Rare spices:** 30 acorns per container, exotic cooking ingredients
- **Luxury goods:** Various items, 40 acorns each, status symbols

### **Possible Distributions**
- **Full Success:** Characters get 70-100% of cache value
- **Negotiated Split:** Characters get 30-50% of cache value
- **Legal Resolution:** Characters get 10-20% as reward
- **Failure:** Characters get nothing but avoid legal trouble

### **Non-Monetary Rewards**
- **Reputation:** Standing with crime families, law enforcement, or community
- **Contacts:** New relationships with NPCs for future adventures
- **Information:** Knowledge about smuggling routes, corruption, or opportunities
- **Favors:** IOUs from grateful or impressed NPCs

## **GM Notes**

### **Pacing Tips**
- **Scene 1:** Let players explore and discover naturally
- **Scene 2:** Use Granny as exposition and world-building
- **Scene 3:** Build tension gradually, allow multiple solutions
- **Scene 4:** Adapt based on previous choices, maintain consequences
- **Scene 5:** Ensure satisfying resolution, set up future adventures

### **Adaptation Notes**
- **Fewer Players:** Reduce NPC numbers, simplify negotiations
- **More Players:** Add complications, multiple simultaneous scenes
- **Different Tone:** Adjust violence level, moral complexity, humor
- **Time Constraints:** Focus on key scenes, streamline exploration

### **World-Building Opportunities**
- **Species Interactions:** Show different cultural approaches to problems
- **Economic Reality:** Demonstrate how much money matters to working creatures
- **Urban Environment:** Highlight infrastructure, neighborhoods, daily life
- **Social Dynamics:** Explore class, crime, law, and community relationships

### **Success Metrics**
The adventure succeeds if players:
- Feel immersed in The Great Burrow's unique world
- Make meaningful choices with clear consequences
- Interact with diverse, memorable NPCs
- Experience the tension between survival and morality
- Want to continue exploring this setting

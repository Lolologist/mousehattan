# **Welcome to The Great Burrow**
## *Player Handout for "The Smuggler's Cache" One-Shot*

---

## **The Forest Metropolis**

In the heart of an ancient mixed hardwood forest, where massive oaks drop their bounty and maples paint autumn gold, lies The Great Burrow - a sprawling metropolis of woodland creatures that rivals any great city in scope, ambition, and complexity. This is a city where squirrels build rope bridges between skyscrapers of bark and leaf, where beaver-engineered dams power workshops that never sleep, where organized crime families of rats control the shadows while mice cling to old money and older traditions.

Your species, your burrow, and your branch height may determine your starting point - but in a city built by tiny paws with impossible ambition, every creature has a chance to climb higher, dig deeper, or disappear entirely into the spaces between.

---

## **The Five Burrows**

### **Mousehattan - The Vertical Metropolis**
Rising from a rocky outcropping where ancient oak roots penetrate stone, Mousehattan is the beating heart of commerce and ambition. The Acorn Exchange trading floor sets prices that ripple across the forest, while Wall Street (literally carved into walls) houses the financial powers that can make or break a creature's fortune. Old mouse families with their precise, articulated speech clash with new rat money - loud, confident entrepreneurs who "make holes bigger." The Longtail and Whisker crime families control the shadows with codes like "the family provides," ensuring everyone pays their "insurance."

### **The Branchx - The Canopy Commons**
High in the ancient grove where oak and maple create natural highways, The Branchx sprawls through three dimensions of working-class pride. Here, your branch height literally determines your social status - "high branch, warm winter" as they say. The Nutcracker Union ensures construction crews get their due, while chipmunk couriers race through territories divided between old squirrel families and crow-affiliated gangs. Hawks patrol the daylight, making every trip a calculated risk that forces covered routes and buddy systems.

### **The Hives - The Agricultural Innovation District**
In sunny meadow clearings, The Hives showcases what cooperation between species can achieve. Ancient rabbit warrens control the honey trade while guinea pig immigrants bring revolutionary terracing techniques and their musical, rolling speech patterns. Shrews in flat caps whistle ultrasonic commands to beetle workforces, managing everything from composting facilities to silk pavilions where moths are cultivated for luxury textiles. This is the most diverse burrow, where "every bee has its flower" and honey flows like gold.

### **Lin's Brook - The Working Waterfront**
Where Whisker Creek meets tidal waters, Lin's Brook embodies blue-collar pride and aquatic expertise. The Great Dam and its secondary structures power workshops around the clock, while beaver lodge complexes house multi-generational families who judge others by the quality of their work. Otter clans run the docks with their fluid, expressive speech full of water metaphors, operating ferry services and competitive slide-racing venues. When giants come to drink or fish, the entire burrow knows the evacuation slides by heart.

### **Scurry Island - The Forgotten Burrow**
Built on and around "The Old Giant," a massive fallen oak, Scurry Island sits isolated in the central clearing. Connected only by three unreliable ferries (two usually broken), it's become the black market capital where opossums master the art of "playing dead" to society while thriving in the shadows. Raccoons run sophisticated fence operations, literally "washing" stolen goods with their dexterous paws. The Ranger Station houses elite warriors who venture into the Deep Woods to face titans - "between the city and the dark" as their oath states.

## **The Creatures of The Great Burrow**

### **Core Species & Their Cultures**

**Mice**: Old money families with precise speech and formal manners, descendants of the original wall-carvers who believe in "proper" ways of doing everything. They speak softly but expect to be heard, using formal titles even in casual conversation.

**Rats**: New money entrepreneurs with loud, confident speech who "make holes bigger" and network aggressively across species lines. They interrupt frequently, use superlatives constantly, and aren't afraid to make enemies while making fortunes.

**Squirrels**: Height-obsessed traditionalists whose branch level indicates social status, speaking quickly with dropped consonants when excited. Natural hoarders who judge others by winter storage and take pride in rope bridge craftsmanship.

**Chipmunks**: Speed-focused courier culture with rapid-fire speech and route-number slang, forming tight crews that become chosen families. They use hand gestures extensively and often finish each other's sentences.

**Beavers**: Master builders who judge others by work quality, speaking in deliberate construction metaphors with measured cadences. They're suspicious of anything "artisanal" and take pride in practical problem-solving.

**Otters**: Waterfront workers with fluid, expressive speech full of water metaphors, competitive about everything especially sliding. They speak in overlapping conversations and use playful teasing even in serious discussions.

**Rabbits**: Agricultural aristocracy measuring wealth in hives, speaking in collective terms with warm, measured speech patterns. Large families are status symbols, and they're incredibly protective of trade secrets.

**Guinea Pigs**: Recent immigrants from "Beyond the Southern Meadows" with musical, rolling speech and revolutionary terracing techniques. They use collective "we" instead of "I" and frequently switch between Common Forest and their native dialect.

**Opossums**: Survivalist philosophers who "play dead" to society while thriving in shadows, masters of saying one thing while meaning another. They speak slowly with folksy sayings and dark humor.

**Raccoons**: Clever operators who literally "wash" stolen goods, smooth and persuasive with lots of hand gestures and euphemisms. They're excellent at code-switching between different social groups.

## **Daily Life & Survival**

### **The Rhythm of Urban Forest Life**
- **Dawn**: Predator danger highest, work begins carefully
- **Morning**: Commutes via the Great Underground subway system
- **Midday**: Safest surface travel time, peak commerce
- **Afternoon**: School, apprenticeships, social time  
- **Dusk**: Race to safety, second predator peak
- **Night**: Underground entertainment, black market hours

### **The Titans That Walk**
Bears could cause total evacuations and physically destroy entire districts. Deer movements reshape landscapes, and their antler rubs can devastate trees. Moose are rarest but most feared - their feeding can topple ancient oaks. These giants aren't malevolent, they simply exist at a scale that makes coexistence nearly impossible. The Great Burrow has evolved elaborate warning systems, evacuation protocols, and offering stations to minimize catastrophic encounters.

### **Predator Reality**
Hawks control daytime surface travel, forcing covered routes and buddy systems. Owls are silent death in the night shift. Snakes create seasonal terror in the canopy. Every creature knows the alarm signals, and "never travel alone" is ingrained from birth.

### **The Underground Economy**
Crime families offer protection from worse fates, running "insurance" operations that often provide actual security services. Everything's available for a price on Scurry Island and in underground networks. Some places remain neutral ground, and burrow guards have varying effectiveness and corruption levels across different territories.

Rangers and some titan survivors are rumored to possess uncanny abilities - impossible luck, perfect timing, or moments of inexplicable strength - though most citizens dismiss these as tall tales born from trauma and wishful thinking.

## **Character Concepts & Backgrounds**

### **The Climber**
An ambitious creature seeking to rise above their station, whether that's a ground-level squirrel dreaming of high branches, a working-class mouse aiming for Wall Street, or a Scurry Island exile planning their return to respectability.

### **The Fixer**
Connected to multiple burrows and communities, you solve problems others can't or won't handle. Maybe you're a chipmunk courier who knows every shortcut, a weasel information broker, or a ferret deal-maker who can get anything for anyone.

### **The Traditionalist**
You represent the old ways in a rapidly changing city. Perhaps you're a beaver master craftsman watching artisanal shops mock your family's techniques, a rabbit beekeeper facing guinea pig innovations, or a mouse from an ancient family seeing rats buy their way into power.

### **The Outsider**
You don't quite fit the mold of your species or burrow. Maybe you're a rat who prefers quiet negotiation to loud deals, a squirrel who works at ground level, or a creature who's moved between burrows and sees the bigger picture.

### **The Survivor**
Life in The Great Burrow has made you tough and resourceful. You might be a Scurry Island native who knows how to disappear, a Branchx construction worker who's survived multiple hawk attacks, or a creature who's lived through titan encounters.

### **The Idealist**
You believe The Great Burrow can be better and you're willing to work for change. Perhaps you're a union organizer fighting gentrification, a young creature promoting inter-species cooperation, or someone working to bridge the gaps between burrows.

## **About "The Smuggler's Cache"**

You'll be playing creatures who find themselves involved with a hidden cache of valuable contraband in Lin's Brook's gentrifying Greenbank district. This isn't just about treasure - it's about competing interests, moral choices, and the complex web of relationships that hold The Great Burrow together. You might be:

- **Hired investigators** looking into missing goods
- **Accidental discoverers** who stumbled onto something valuable
- **Community members** concerned about suspicious activity
- **Opportunists** seeking to improve your circumstances
- **Professionals** with skills that make you useful to various parties

The adventure will test your ability to navigate urban politics, species relationships, and the gray areas between legal and illegal, survival and morality. Your choices will affect not just your own fortunes, but the ongoing struggles between tradition and change, community and profit, that define life in the forest metropolis.

Remember: In The Great Burrow, everyone's connected to someone, every action has consequences, and there's always another creature trying to take what's yours. But if you can make it here, you can make it anywhere in the forest.

**Welcome to the city that never sleeps, where the giants never care, and where small creatures dream big dreams in the shadow of ancient oaks.**

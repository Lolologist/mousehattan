## **Group Dynamics & Relationships**

### **The Network**
These six characters form a loose network of contacts who occasionally work together on opportunities that require diverse skills:

**Finn & Gilda:** Professional relationship from waterfront construction work. Finn provides waterfront access and physical labor, Gilda handles technical engineering problems.

**Finn & Patch:** Mutual assistance relationship. Patch helped Finn avoid trouble with dock supervisors, while Finn provides Patch with waterfront information.

**Gilda & Clover:** Business relationship. Gilda has designed processing equipment for Clover's agricultural operations, creating a technical/agricultural partnership.

**Patch & Bramble:** Information trading partnership. They share tips about trade routes and opportunities, with Patch providing street intelligence and Bramble offering legitimate business insights.

**Bramble & Slink:** Mutual respect despite different methods. Bramble appreciates Slink's determination in protecting workers, while Slink values Bramble's community organizing skills.

**Clover & Slink:** Professional relationship. Slink has provided security for Clover's agricultural operations, creating trust between the community organizer and the investigator.

### **Why They're Together**
The group has come together for "The Smuggler's Cache" because:

1. **Finn** heard rumors about the cache through his waterfront connections and ferry system knowledge
2. **Gilda** was hired to assess the warehouse's structural integrity for potential development
3. **Patch** sensed an opportunity through her information network and titan-touched intuition
4. **Clover** is investigating disruptions to legitimate agricultural trade routes
5. **Bramble** wants to understand how smuggling affects working-class dock workers
6. **Slink** was hired to investigate the missing smuggling operation as part of a larger case

Each has their own reasons for being interested, but they recognize that working together gives them better odds of success and survival.

### **Character Motivations**

**Finn:** Needs extra income to supplement dock work, sees cache as opportunity for financial security and adventure

**Gilda:** Curious about the engineering aspects of the smuggling operation, plus the money would fund her independent workshop dreams

**Patch:** Interested in the information value and connections the cache represents, plus her titan-touched abilities draw her to significant events

**Clover:** Wants to understand how smuggling affects legitimate agricultural trade and her community's prosperity

**Bramble:** Investigating how illegal operations impact working creatures and union interests

**Slink:** Following the investigation wherever it leads, plus the cache could provide leverage for future cases and contacts

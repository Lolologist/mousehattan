# **Additional Adventure Hooks for The Great Burrow**

## **1. Burrow-Specific Adventure Hooks**

### **Mousehattan Adventures**

#### **The Acorn Futures Conspiracy**
The Acorn Exchange experiences wild fluctuations as someone manipulates futures contracts for the upcoming harvest. Characters discover a network of corrupt traders working with external bandits to create artificial scarcity. The investigation leads through exclusive Wall Street clubs, midnight meetings in Sub-Basement speakeasies, and eventually to a warehouse where stolen acorns are being hoarded. Time pressure mounts as the conspiracy threatens to crash the economy before winter, leaving thousands without food stores.

#### **The Lost Vault of the First Families**
When renovations in Times Squeak uncover an ancient sealed tunnel, whispers spread of the legendary First Vault - where the founding mouse families supposedly stored their greatest treasures. Multiple parties race to find it: old money families claiming ancestral rights, new rat entrepreneurs seeking legitimacy, and the Longtail crime family who believe it contains evidence of their historical betrayal. Characters must navigate Mousehattan's literal and social underground while dodging cave-ins, ancient traps, and modern thugs.

#### **The Vertical Territory War**
A young rat tech entrepreneur purchases prime upper-level real estate traditionally reserved for old mouse families, sparking a shadow war. Sabotage, social warfare, and "accidents" escalate as both sides hire muscle. Characters get pulled in when they witness a suspicious elevator failure and must choose sides or broker peace before the conflict spills into open violence that could destroy Mousehattan's carefully maintained social order.

#### **The Firefly Shortage Crisis**
Times Squeak's managed firefly lighting system fails when the insects mysteriously stop responding to their handlers. In the darkness, crime skyrockets and panic spreads. Characters investigate whether it's natural phenomena, sabotage by light-control unions, or something more sinister while navigating a Mousehattan plunged into dangerous darkness where predators and criminals thrive equally.

### **The Branchx Adventures**

#### **The Corvid Protection Racket**
A murder of crows offers "protection" to Branchx residents - for a price. Those who refuse find themselves harassed by coordinated crow attacks during surface travel. Characters must determine if this is simple extortion or if the corvids are responding to some deeper threat. The investigation reveals complex politics between corvid factions and hints at their unknowable agenda while characters balance negotiation with the alien intelligence against protecting their community.

#### **The Ghost Bridges Mystery**
Construction crews report that new bridges are appearing overnight - expertly crafted spans that no union claims to have built. Some see it as a blessing, others fear a trap. Characters investigating discover the bridges are built to specifications that don't match any known engineering tradition. The mystery deepens when creatures using these bridges report strange dreams and uncanny luck, leading to questions about who or what is trying to help The Branchx - and why.

#### **The Branch Rights Rebellion**
Young squirrels fed up with height hierarchy organize to seize prime upper branches from established families. The movement starts with peaceful protests but escalates to sabotage and occupied trees. Characters must navigate between revolutionary fervor and traditional authority while uncovering that outside agitators from Mousehattan are funding both sides to destabilize The Branchx for a massive gentrification plan.

#### **The Underground Railroad Rescue**
The abandoned tunnel system beneath The Branchx becomes an escape route for creatures fleeing debt and crime family obligations. When a group of refugees goes missing in the forgotten sections, their families hire characters to find them. The search reveals that something else lives in those dark tunnels - feral creatures who've adapted to permanent underground life and don't welcome visitors. Characters must rescue the refugees while uncovering a hidden society with its own brutal rules.

### **The Hives Adventures**

#### **The Beetle Revolution**
Managed beetle workforces across The Hives begin displaying unprecedented coordination and intelligence, refusing shrew commands and organizing their own activities. As agricultural production grinds to halt, characters investigate whether this is natural evolution, magical interference, or the result of guinea pig experiments in beetle communication gone wrong. The situation becomes dire when beetles begin constructing their own structures and defending them against creature intrusion.

#### **The Democratic Crisis**
The Hives' first free election turns chaotic when multiple ballot boxes go missing and several candidates claim victory. Prairie dog tradition demands consensus, but rabbit families insist on their historical privileges, while guinea pig innovators push for modern voting methods. Characters must find the missing ballots while preventing the situation from exploding into inter-species violence that could destroy The Hives' fragile democratic experiment.

#### **The Honey Plague**
A mysterious illness strikes The Hives' bee populations, threatening the honey economy. Traditional rabbit beekeepers blame guinea pig innovative methods, while prairie dogs suspect sabotage from competitors. Characters race to find the source while managing panic, preventing economic collapse, and discovering that the plague might be connected to new plants introduced by well-meaning but naive mountain marmots.

#### **The Caravan Disappearance**
An important trade caravan from the Pine Barrens vanishes just before reaching The Hives. The cargo included vital winter supplies and diplomatic gifts meant to cement a crucial alliance. Characters must brave the dangerous borderlands, deal with bandit groups, and uncover whether this was simple robbery or part of a larger plot to isolate The Great Burrow from its trading partners.

### **Lin's Brook Adventures**

#### **The Dam Saboteur**
A series of precisely targeted attacks weakens key dam structures during storm season. The saboteur knows exactly where to strike for maximum damage while avoiding detection. Characters must protect the dams while investigating whether this is the work of anti-beaver radicals, Silverclaw Family enforcement gone wrong, or something more sinister. Time pressure mounts as storm season approaches and one more failure could flood half of Lin's Brook.

#### **The Otter Sliding Championship Scandal**
The annual sliding championships turn deadly when several competitors suffer suspicious accidents. What seems like rivalry gone too far reveals a deeper conspiracy involving gambling debts, fixed races, and a new sliding wax that might be more dangerous than anyone realizes. Characters must navigate otter clan politics while protecting athletes and uncovering who benefits from turning sport into blood sport.

#### **The Greenbank Gentrification War**
Artisanal shops and craft breweries clash with traditional working families as gentrification accelerates. When mysterious fires target both old businesses and new developments, each side blames the other. Characters discover that someone is playing both sides, using the conflict to cover a massive real estate scheme that would transform Lin's Brook into a playground for the wealthy while displacing thousands.

#### **The Nightshift Disappearances**
Workers on the graveyard shift at the mills and workshops begin vanishing without a trace. The only clue: strange marks near machinery that look like neither predator attacks nor accidents. Characters working the nightshift themselves discover that something is hunting in the darkness between the machinery - something that might have been created by the very innovations that power Lin's Brook's industry.

### **Scurry Island Adventures**

#### **The Ranger Recruitment Test**
Characters are invited to try out for the elite Rangers through a series of increasingly dangerous tests. What starts as physical challenges evolves into moral dilemmas when candidates must choose between following orders and saving lives. The final test reveals that the Rangers are planning something big - a preemptive strike against a titan threat that could either save or doom the city.

#### **The Old Giant's Secret**
Deep exploration of "The Old Giant" fallen oak that forms Scurry Island's foundation reveals sealed chambers that predate The Great Burrow. Inside, evidence suggests this was once home to a different civilization. As characters delve deeper, they awaken something that's been sleeping in the heartwood. The race is on to reseal the chambers before ancient history destroys the present.

#### **The Fence War**
Competition between raccoon fence operations erupts into open warfare when a new player offers to "wash" goods at half price. Characters hired to investigate discover the new operation is a front for something worse - they're not just laundering stolen goods but trafficking information that could compromise The Great Burrow's defenses. Players must navigate the criminal underworld while preventing a security catastrophe.

#### **The Black Market Medicine Crisis**
A new "miracle cure" floods Scurry Island's black market, promising to heal titan-encounter trauma. Desperate creatures pay anything for relief, but users begin displaying strange behaviors and supernatural abilities. Characters trace the source to discover someone is harvesting materials from titan encounters and processing them into dangerous drugs that might transform users into something inhuman.

## **2. Cross-Burrow Adventures**

#### **The Great Underground Collapse**
A catastrophic failure in the tunnel system traps hundreds of commuters from all five burrows in a sealed section between stations. Characters among the trapped must organize survival, manage panic, and investigate whether this was an accident or sabotage while above ground, the city faces its first day without functional mass transit. The adventure explores class and species tensions in crisis while racing against time before air runs out.

#### **The Inter-Burrow Conspiracy**
Representatives from each burrow begin dying in seemingly unrelated accidents. Characters hired to protect a surviving representative uncover a conspiracy to destroy the Five-Burrow Coalition and establish a military dictatorship "for protection against titans." The plot involves creatures from every burrow who believe democracy has failed, forcing characters to navigate complex politics while preventing a coup.

#### **The Touring Theatre Troupe Mystery**
A famous theatre company touring all five burrows leaves chaos in its wake - thefts, disappearances, and social upheaval. Characters hired as security discover the troupe are revolutionaries using performances to spread radical ideas and coordinate resistance cells. Players must decide whether to stop them, join them, or find a middle path while navigating the cultural differences of each burrow.

#### **The Acorn Blight Investigation**
When acorn supplies in all five burrows show signs of the same mysterious blight, panic spreads about winter starvation. Characters race between burrows following clues, discovering the blight isn't natural but targeted. The investigation reveals someone is trying to make The Great Burrow dependent on imported food, setting up economic control by outside forces.

## **3. Seasonal Adventure Ideas**

### **Spring - The Flood Festival**

#### **The Rising Waters**
Unprecedented spring melt threatens all riverside districts. Characters coordinate evacuation efforts while investigating why flood predictions failed so badly. They discover someone has been altering upstream dams, either through incompetence or malice. The adventure combines rescue operations with investigation as waters rise hourly and different burrows blame each other for the crisis.

#### **Mating Season Madness**
Spring hormones create chaos as traditional mating grounds overlap with new urban development. Characters must manage conflicts between instinct and civilization while investigating why this year's season seems particularly intense. The discovery of pheromone-enhancing substances being sold leads to a black market operation exploiting natural urges for profit.

#### **The Awakening Sabotage**
The Great Awakening festival celebrating spring gets targeted by extremists who believe civilization has lost touch with natural cycles. Characters provide security while investigating threats, ultimately discovering the extremists may be responding to a real concern - something ancient is stirring with the spring that the city has forgotten how to appease.

### **Summer - The Harvest Preparation**

#### **The Drought Conspiracy**
An unusual summer drought stresses water supplies, but characters discover evidence the shortage is artificial. Someone is diverting water for private reservoirs, preparing to control supply when desperation peaks. The investigation leads through all five burrows as players uncover a network preparing to hold the city hostage.

#### **The Tourist Invasion**
Summer brings creatures from across the forest to see The Great Burrow. When several tourists die in suspicious circumstances, xenophobia threatens to destroy the tourism economy many depend on. Characters must solve the murders while managing cultural conflicts and discovering that someone wants The Great Burrow isolated from the wider forest.

#### **The Heatwave Hallucinations**
Extreme heat brings reports of shared visions - creatures across the city dream of the same ancient forest, untouched by civilization. As heat exhaustion cases mount, characters investigate whether this is mass hysteria, environmental poisoning, or genuine spiritual communication. The dreams grow more urgent, warning of a choice the city must make.

### **Fall - The Harvest Rush**

#### **The Squirrel Storage Wars**
Competition for prime winter storage space turns violent as traditional locations overflow. Characters hired to guard a storage facility discover systematic theft that goes beyond normal pilfering. The investigation reveals a coordinated effort to control winter food supplies, potentially dooming thousands to starvation.

#### **The Harvest Festival Disaster**
The great Harvest Festival celebrating inter-burrow cooperation faces disaster when shipments between burrows get hijacked. Characters race to recover stolen goods while uncovering that the thefts are cover for smuggling something more dangerous - titan pheromones that could draw giants directly to the city.

#### **The Early Winter Panic**
When signs point to winter arriving weeks early, panic buying and hoarding threaten social order. Characters investigate whether weather patterns are natural while managing civil unrest. They discover climate manipulation experiments gone wrong, forcing choices between exposing the truth and maintaining order.

### **Winter - The Long Dark**

#### **The Heating Fuel Crisis**
Critical heating fuel reserves vanish from multiple burrows simultaneously. As temperatures plummet, characters race to find the stolen supplies before creatures freeze. The investigation reveals the theft connects to a protection racket offering "guaranteed" warmth for those who pay, run by a coalition crossing crime family boundaries.

#### **The Winter Madness**
Isolation and darkness bring reports of creatures acting strangely - abandoning families, destroying food stores, walking into the frozen forest. Characters investigating discover a pattern suggesting something is calling vulnerable minds. The source traces to ancient sites buried under snow, awakening as the city grows larger and more complex.

#### **The Ice Bridge Incident**
Unprecedented cold freezes the rivers solid, creating natural bridges to areas normally accessible only by ferry. When creatures start using these bridges and disappearing, characters investigate what's hunting on the ice. They discover the bridges lead to territories claimed by exiled creatures who see winter as their chance for revenge.

## **4. Character-Driven Hooks Based on Species**

### **Mouse Character Hooks**

#### **The Inheritance Trap**
Your ancient family wall-rights are challenged by newly discovered documents. To retain your heritage, you must prove your lineage through a series of trials that reveal family secrets better left buried. Other mouse families circle like predators, ready to claim what you might lose.

#### **The New Money Mentor**
A successful rat entrepreneur offers to mentor you in "modern" business methods, scandalizing traditional mouse society. The opportunity promises wealth but risks family ostracism. When the mentor's enemies target you, survival means embracing methods your ancestors would abhor.

#### **The Lost Tunnel Mystery**
Your grandfather's journal references family tunnels hidden during the Species Wars. Finding them could restore your family's fortune, but other mice families and crime organizations are searching too. The tunnels' location ties to shameful family history during the conflicts.

### **Rat Character Hooks**

#### **The Hostile Takeover**
Your startup threatens established mouse family businesses, triggering economic warfare. Sabotage, social ostracism, and legal challenges mount as you fight to "make the hole bigger." Success means changing Mousehattan forever; failure means joining the countless rats who dreamed too big.

#### **The Family Defector**
You flee your crime family seeking legitimate success, but the family doesn't let go easily. Building a new life means staying ahead of enforcers while resisting pressure to use criminal methods for business advantage. Your insider knowledge makes you valuable to law enforcement and criminals alike.

#### **The Bridge Builder**
Your vision of rat-mouse cooperation in business faces resistance from both species. Creating the city's first truly integrated company requires navigating prejudice, tradition, and active sabotage from those who profit from division. Success could transform The Great Burrow's economy.

### **Squirrel Character Hooks**

#### **The Branch Revolutionary**
You reject height hierarchy, organizing young squirrels to challenge traditional branch rights. Your movement threatens established order, making you a target for conservative families and those who profit from the current system. Revolution requires allies from unexpected places.

#### **The Lost Grove Legacy**
Your family's ancestral grove, destroyed in development, supposedly hid seeds from the First Trees. Finding and planting them could restore your family's status, but developers, crime families, and even Rangers have interest in keeping the past buried.

#### **The Corvid Touched**
After an encounter with corvids, you understand fragments of their communication. This gift/curse makes you valuable to those seeking to negotiate with the alien intelligence but marks you as "unnatural" to traditional squirrels. The corvids have their own plans for you.

### **Chipmunk Character Hooks**

#### **The Courier Conspiracy**
Your delivery route gives you access to secrets powerful creatures would kill to protect. When you accidentally deliver the wrong package, you're pulled into a conspiracy that threatens your courier crew family. Speed isn't enough when everyone knows your routes.

#### **The Crew Betrayal**
Your courier crew gets framed for stealing high-value deliveries. Proving innocence means uncovering who benefits from discrediting chipmunk courier services. The investigation reveals plans to replace traditional couriers with new technology that would destroy your way of life.

#### **The Underground Racing Champion**
Your speed in illegal tunnel races attracts dangerous attention. Crime families want to fix races, while legitimate businesses offer sponsorship if you go public. Choosing your path determines whether you lift chipmunk courier culture up or sell it out.

### **Beaver Character Hooks**

#### **The Construction Whistleblower**
You discover critical infrastructure uses substandard materials that will fail catastrophically. Exposing the truth means challenging your guild, your family's reputation, and powerful interests who profited. Staying quiet makes you complicit in inevitable disaster.

#### **The Innovation Heretic**
Your new construction techniques could revolutionize building but violate sacred beaver traditions. Pursuing your vision means exile from traditional beaver society while proving your methods work. Success transforms the building industry; failure confirms you're a dangerous radical.

#### **The Dam Keeper's Burden**
You inherit responsibility for ancient dams holding more than just water - they seal something that should stay buried. Modern development threatens these safeguards, forcing you to choose between progress and catastrophic consequences only your family remembers.

### **Guinea Pig Character Hooks**

#### **The Agricultural Revolution**
Your innovative farming techniques threaten established rabbit dynasties who suppress methods that could feed thousands more. Spreading your knowledge means underground teaching, avoiding "accidents," and building coalitions between desperate creatures and forward-thinking allies.

#### **The Cultural Bridge**
Your ability to code-switch between guinea pig community and mainstream society makes you an ideal mediator but trusted by neither fully. When cultural conflicts threaten violence, your unique position becomes crucial for preventing tragedy - if you can survive being in the middle.

#### **The Mountain Secret**
Your family carries knowledge from the homeland about surviving giants - knowledge that could save The Great Burrow but challenges everything creatures believe about titans. Revealing this means confronting both those who profit from fear and those who've built their identity on current methods.

### **Otter Character Hooks**

#### **The River Rebellion**
You lead dock workers demanding fair wages and safer conditions, challenging both the Silverclaw Family and established business interests. Your movement could transform Lin's Brook or be crushed in blood. The river knows how to carry away bodies and secrets equally well.

#### **The Sliding Prodigy**
Your natural talent for competitive sliding draws sponsors and criminals equally. Refusing to fix races makes you a target; accepting makes you wealthy but betrays everything otters value. Your choices will define whether the sport remains pure or becomes another corrupted entertainment.

#### **The Water Whisperer**
You sense changes in water others miss - pollution, tampering, or worse. This gift reveals someone is systematically poisoning The Great Burrow's water supply. Investigating means challenging powers who've kept you and yours "in your place" while racing against time before the damage becomes irreversible.

## **5. Long-Form Campaign Arcs**

### **The Titan Prophecy**
*Length: 20-30 sessions*

Ancient texts suggest titans aren't random forces but follow patterns readable by those who know how. Characters discover a prophecy predicting a "Great Convergence" where multiple titans will arrive simultaneously, potentially destroying The Great Burrow. The campaign involves:
- Searching all five burrows for prophecy fragments
- Gaining trust of spiritual movements who guard titan knowledge
- Expeditions into Deep Forest to observe titan behavior
- Political intrigue as some seek to exploit prophecy for power
- Ultimate choice: evacuate the city, attempt to redirect titans, or discover the prophecy's true meaning
- Revelation that titans respond to the city's growth, leading to fundamental questions about The Great Burrow's future

### **The Underground Empire**
*Length: 25-35 sessions*

The Great Underground holds more than just transit tunnels. Characters discover evidence of a vast civilization predating The Great Burrow, with sealed sections holding treasures, dangers, and answers to why this location became such a nexus of activity. The campaign features:
- Mapping forgotten tunnel systems across all burrows
- Deciphering ancient writing that predates known creature languages
- Encounters with devolved descendants of the original builders
- Corporate interests seeking to exploit discoveries
- Crime families fighting over ancient territories
- Revelation that the previous civilization fell to something still present in the depths
- Choice to seal dangerous knowledge or risk using it to protect the modern city

### **The Species Integration Movement**
*Length: 20-30 sessions*

Young creatures across all species organize to break down traditional barriers, facing resistance from established powers who profit from division. Characters become leaders or opponents of this movement that could transform or destroy The Great Burrow's social fabric. The campaign includes:
- Organizing cross-species unions and mutual aid networks
- Protecting mixed-species families from harassment
- Investigating false flag operations designed to inflame tensions
- Building coalitions between progressive elements in each burrow
- Surviving assassination attempts and frame jobs
- Political campaigns to change fundamental laws
- Ultimate confrontation between old and new ways that determines The Great Burrow's future character

### **The Economic Revolution**
*Length: 15-25 sessions*

The acorn standard collapses when synthetic alternatives flood the market, triggering economic chaos that threatens civilization itself. Characters navigate this transformation while various factions attempt to shape what replaces the old system. The campaign explores:
- Black market operations keeping creatures fed during shortage
- Corporate wars over new currency standards
- Underground economies based on barter and mutual aid
- Crime family adaptation to new power structures
- International implications as trade relationships collapse
- Discovery that the synthetic acorns have troubling origins
- Opportunity to build a more equitable economy or entrench existing inequalities

### **The Ranger Independence War**
*Length: 20-30 sessions*

When Rangers declare Scurry Island an independent city-state, The Great Burrow faces its first civil war. Characters must choose sides or broker peace while titans and predators exploit the chaos. The campaign features:
- Military operations in urban and wilderness settings
- Diplomatic missions to prevent burrow-by-burrow fragmentation
- Discovering the real reasons behind Ranger rebellion
- Managing civilian populations caught in conflict
- Moral choices between security and freedom
- External enemies exploiting internal division
- Resolution that redefines the relationship between military and civilian authority

### **The Climate Crisis Chronicles**
*Length: 30-40 sessions*

Unprecedented weather changes threaten The Great Burrow's survival as infrastructure fails, food systems collapse, and creatures turn on each other. Characters lead adaptation efforts while investigating whether changes are natural or creature-made. The extended campaign includes:
- Seasonal disasters requiring immediate response
- Long-term planning for city adaptation
- Expeditions to discover climate causes
- Political battles over resource allocation
- Technological innovations versus traditional solutions
- Mass migration management as surrounding areas become uninhabitable
- Discovery of ancient climate manipulation tools
- Fundamental choices about growth, sustainability, and survival
- Building new alliances with previously hostile forest communities
- Transformation of The Great Burrow into something that can survive the new world

---

## **Using These Hooks**

These adventure ideas are designed to:
- Showcase each burrow's unique character
- Explore the setting's central tensions
- Provide variety in tone and scale
- Support different play styles
- Build on established lore while adding new elements
- Create opportunities for meaningful character choices
- Address themes relevant to both the forest setting and urban life

Remember that The Great Burrow thrives on the balance between whimsical woodland charm and gritty urban realism. Every adventure should reflect both the wonder of creatures achieving the impossible and the harsh realities of survival in a dangerous world.

The best campaigns will weave multiple hooks together, letting character actions in one adventure create consequences that spawn the next. The city never sleeps, titans never care, and there's always another story waiting in the shadows between the burrows.

# **Campaign & Adventure Ideas for The Great Burrow**

## **Full Campaigns (20+ Sessions)**

### **The Titan's Shadow**
A massive bear has taken up residence in the northern forests, closer to the city than any giant has been in decades. Its presence disrupts trade routes, causes mass evacuations, and splits the city between those who want to appease it and those who demand action. The characters become involved when they discover the bear isn't acting naturally - something is driving it toward the city. The campaign explores titan ecology, Ranger politics, ancient forest spirits, and culminates in either a desperate battle or a profound understanding that changes how the city relates to the giants forever.

### **The Great Gentrification War**
Mousehattan money is systematically buying up traditional neighborhoods across all burrows, displacing working families and erasing cultural heritage. What starts as isolated protests escalates into a city-wide conflict between old and new money, traditional families and entrepreneurs, workers and investors. The characters find themselves at the center of this struggle, uncovering corruption, organizing resistance, and ultimately deciding what kind of city The Great Burrow will become. Features union organizing, crime family politics, inter-burrow diplomacy, and the question of whether progress must come at the cost of community.

### **The Underground Conspiracy**
The Great Underground system is failing - not from age, but from sabotage. Tunnels collapse, trains derail, and creatures disappear in the depths. Investigation reveals a conspiracy involving corrupt mole engineers, bandit infiltrators, and a secret society that wants to return the forest to its "natural" state by destroying the city's infrastructure. The campaign takes characters through every burrow's underground sections, into the abandoned tunnel systems, and finally into a vast network of caves where the conspirators plan to bring down the entire subway system during rush hour.

### **The Species Integration Crisis**
A new disease affects only certain species, creating panic and threatening to tear apart the city's multi-species cooperation. Quarantines turn into segregation, old prejudices resurface, and some creatures begin calling for species-only territories. The characters must navigate medical mysteries, political intrigue, and social unrest while uncovering the truth: the disease is artificial, created by extremists who want to "purify" the burrows. The campaign explores themes of prejudice, cooperation, and what it truly means to be a unified city.

### **The Ranger Rebellion**
Scurry Island declares independence from The Great Burrow, with the Rangers refusing to protect a city that treats them as expendable outcasts. Without Ranger protection, titan encounters become catastrophic, trade routes fall to bandits, and the city faces existential threats. The characters must choose sides in a conflict that pits military necessity against civilian authority, survival against democracy. The campaign features wilderness adventures, political negotiations, military action, and ultimately the question of who has the right to rule in a world where giants walk.

### **The Acorn Crash**
The acorn-based economy collapses when a blight destroys the oak groves, sending the city into economic chaos. Traditional power structures crumble as creatures struggle to survive, crime families war over dwindling resources, and desperate measures become commonplace. The characters navigate this post-economic world while investigating the blight's true cause - sabotage by external enemies who want to destroy The Great Burrow's regional dominance. The campaign combines survival horror, economic intrigue, and ultimately a race to save not just the city's economy but its very existence.

## **Short Campaigns (3-10 Sessions)**

### **The Ferry War**
Scurry Island's ferry system completely breaks down, stranding residents and cutting off the black market that many creatures depend on. Two rival groups emerge with different solutions - one wants to build a permanent bridge, the other wants to establish a new ferry monopoly. The characters become involved when they're hired to investigate sabotage, only to discover that both sides are being manipulated by mainland interests who want to control the island's economy.

### **The Honey Futures Scandal**
Someone is manipulating honey prices on the Acorn Exchange, threatening to bankrupt traditional beekeeping families in The Hives. The investigation leads through Mousehattan's financial district, underground gambling dens, and ultimately to a conspiracy involving crime families, corrupt traders, and a new synthetic honey that could revolutionize or destroy the agricultural economy.

### **The Missing Rangers**
An entire Ranger patrol vanishes in the Deep Woods, and their last transmission mentioned discovering something "impossible." The characters are recruited to find them, leading to encounters with unknown titan species, ancient forest ruins, and a secret that could change everything the city knows about the giants that walk the earth.

### **The Bridge Builders**
The Nutcracker Union goes on strike just as a crucial bridge project needs completion before winter storms. The characters must navigate labor politics, corporate pressure, and sabotage attempts while the city's transportation network hangs in the balance. The campaign explores working-class solidarity, the value of skilled labor, and the consequences of putting profit over safety.

### **The Crow Negotiation**
A murder of crows demands tribute from The Branchx, threatening to make surface travel impossible if their terms aren't met. The characters become intermediaries in negotiations between alien corvid intelligence and mammalian society, uncovering ancient agreements, territorial disputes, and the complex relationship between protection and extortion.

### **The Underground Railroad**
Creatures are disappearing from their homes and jobs, only to surface weeks later with no memory of where they've been. Investigation reveals an underground network helping creatures escape debt, crime family obligations, and family expectations - but someone is using this legitimate service to traffic creatures for darker purposes.

## **One-Shot Adventures**

### **The Acorn Exchange Heist**
The characters are hired to steal (or protect) a shipment of rare acorns during the chaos of harvest season trading. The job seems simple until they discover multiple other groups with the same target, corrupt guards, and a crime family war brewing in the background. Features social infiltration, action sequences, and moral choices about who deserves the valuable cargo.

### **Hawk Day**
An unusually aggressive hawk has taken up residence above a major bridge crossing, trapping dozens of creatures on both sides. The characters must find a way to deal with the predator while managing panicked civilians, limited supplies, and the approaching deadline of a crucial business meeting that could affect the entire city's economy.

### **The Tunnel Rats**
The characters are hired to clear out a section of abandoned tunnel that's been taken over by feral rats. What seems like simple pest control becomes complicated when they discover the rats are organized, intelligent, and protecting something valuable. The adventure explores the line between civilization and wildness, and what creatures will do to survive.

### **Election Day**
It's Inter-Burrow Council election day, and the characters are hired to ensure fair voting in a contested district. They must deal with voter intimidation, ballot stuffing, crime family interference, and a candidate who may not be what they seem. The adventure combines political intrigue with action as democracy hangs in the balance.

### **The Night Market**
The characters attend The Hives' famous night market during firefly season, but their evening of shopping and entertainment is interrupted when someone starts stealing the lighting insects. Without the fireflies, the market descends into chaos and panic. The characters must catch the thief while navigating crowds, protecting civilians, and dealing with the unique challenges of investigating in near-total darkness.

### **Titan Crossing**
A deer needs to cross through the city to reach its seasonal feeding grounds, and the characters are part of the evacuation and safety team. Everything goes wrong when the evacuation routes are sabotaged, forcing the characters to guide panicked civilians to safety while a creature the size of a building walks through their neighborhood.

### **The Smuggler's Cache**
The characters discover a hidden cache of contraband in an abandoned building, but they're not the only ones looking for it. The adventure becomes a three-way race between the characters, crime family enforcers, and desperate smugglers, all while trying to avoid the attention of burrow guards who would arrest everyone involved.

### **The Beetle Rebellion**
The managed beetles in The Hives suddenly stop following shrew commands and begin acting aggressively. The characters must investigate what's causing the normally docile insects to rebel while protecting panicked farmers and preventing an ecological disaster that could destroy the burrow's agricultural economy.

### **The Ferry Rescue**
One of Scurry Island's ferries breaks down in the middle of the river during a storm, with passengers trapped aboard. The characters must mount a rescue operation using whatever resources they can find, dealing with dangerous weather, mechanical challenges, and the possibility that the breakdown wasn't accidental.

### **The Midnight Duel**
Two prominent citizens challenge each other to a duel over a matter of honor, and the characters become involved as seconds, mediators, or investigators trying to prevent bloodshed. The adventure explores themes of reputation, justice, and tradition while building to a tense confrontation that could have far-reaching consequences for both families involved.

## **Campaign Themes**

### **Urban Fantasy**
The Great Burrow as a magical place where small creatures achieve impossible things through cooperation, ingenuity, and determination. Focus on wonder, community, and the magic of civilization.

### **Political Intrigue**
Complex relationships between burrows, species, and social classes create opportunities for espionage, negotiation, and power struggles that determine the city's future.

### **Survival Horror**
The constant threat of titans, predators, and environmental disasters creates tension and forces characters to make difficult choices about risk, sacrifice, and survival.

### **Social Justice**
Exploring themes of inequality, gentrification, workers' rights, and species integration through the lens of characters fighting for a more equitable city.

### **Economic Drama**
The acorn-based economy, trade relationships, and class conflicts provide opportunities for stories about wealth, poverty, and the value of different kinds of work.

### **Environmental Crisis**
Climate change, resource depletion, and the relationship between urban development and natural ecosystems create urgent challenges that require innovative solutions.
